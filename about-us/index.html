Our Story

Four Decades of Clean Carpets and Floors

Ace Cleaning Experts started the way most good local businesses do — with a family, a van, and a determination to do the job right. For over 40 years, we've helped South Jersey families and businesses restore carpets, tile, upholstery and grout — eliminating stubborn dirt, stains and odors along the way. We're still locally owned and operated: not a franchise, not a national call center, just the same commitment to honest pricing and work you can trust.

Veteran-Owned. Family-Operated. Still Local.

We're proud to be a veteran-owned, family-operated business serving the same South Jersey communities we call home. The person who quotes your job is accountable for the crew that shows up to do it — no corporate hand-offs, no surprise fees.

The Same Care We'd Want in Our Own Homes

Every technician is trained on EPA-certified cleaning agents and modern extraction equipment, and every job wraps up with a walkthrough so you know exactly what was done.

What We Solve

Problems Big and Small, Solved Right

Whether it's one stubborn stain or odor you can't get out, a full-home deep clean, or something larger like water damage restoration, we've got 40+ years of experience figuring out the right fix. Our staff keeps learning the newest techniques so every job gets the highest-quality clean at a fair price — and we'll always tell you honestly what your carpet, tile or upholstery actually needs.
