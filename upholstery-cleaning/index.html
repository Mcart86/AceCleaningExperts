Common Questions

Upholstery Cleaning FAQ

Will cleaning shrink or fade my furniture?

We test each fabric first and choose a method suited to it, which is exactly why we avoid the over-wetting that causes shrinking or color bleed with DIY machines.

Can you get pet odor out of a couch?

In most cases, yes. Odor treatment is a standard part of our upholstery process, not an upcharge.

How long does a sofa take to dry?

Most pieces are dry within a few hours thanks to our low-moisture approach and extraction equipment.

Do you clean leather?

Our current process is built for fabric upholstery. Call us and we can talk through what your specific piece needs.
